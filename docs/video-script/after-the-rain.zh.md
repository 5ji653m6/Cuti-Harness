# 《雨后》（After the Rain）

[English](after-the-rain.md) | 中文

5 分钟「短片」总脚本。建议拆成 20 个 15 秒镜头。

**一句话：** 宇航员坠落雨城 → 穿过市场 → 天台对峙 → 飞船升空。一条因果长流，空间声和事件密度都够。

**风格锚：** Blade Runner 2049 + The Creator + Gravity 落地段。

**生成方式：** 同一角色参考图锁定；每镜结尾动作接到下一镜开头；连续编号当 seed continuity。

## 全局负面约束（每条都带上）

No dialogue, no subtitles, no logos, no extra fingers, consistent female astronaut in a weathered white-orange EVA suit with helmet visor up, photoreal, 16:9, 24fps look, anamorphic film grain.

## Act 1 · 坠落（0:00–1:15）· 镜头 01–05

### 01 · 建立 · 云层破口

Aerial wide: a burning re-entry capsule tears a hole through storm clouds above a coastal megacity at dusk. Camera crane down through the cloud break toward the skyline. One move: crane down. Lightning in distant clouds. Photoreal IMAX, 16:9.

### 02 · 坠落跟拍

Medium tracking: the astronaut in a weathered white-orange EVA suit free-falls through rain toward neon towers. Suit tiles glow from heat. Camera falls with her, slight dutch, 35mm. Rain streaks, city lights rising. No cuts.

### 03 · 撞穿雨棚

Low-angle street: she crashes through a corrugated market canopy, water exploding outward, crates scattering left, a hanging lantern swinging right. Camera locked on the wet street looking up. Photoreal, debris in slow-then-normal time. 16:9.

### 04 · 落地

Close-medium: she hits a flooded alley, knees, visor up, rain hammering the helmet. Camera slow dolly in. Steam off the suit. One puddle reflects neon. Photoreal.

### 05 · 站起环顾

Over-shoulder as she stands and turns left to right, revealing a dense night market: tarps, scooters, hanging meat, neon. Camera orbits 90 degrees right around her. Rain continuous.

## Act 2 · 穿越（1:15–3:00）· 镜头 06–12

### 06 · 市场跟拍

Steadicam behind her walking through a crowded wet night market. Vendors, scooters passing on the left, a train rumbling on an elevated track above right. One tracking move forward. Photoreal, 35mm.

### 07 · 左右超车（立体声关键镜）

She steps into a side street. A scooter with no headlight screams past from left foreground to right background, spraying water. Then a heavier truck passes the opposite way, right to left. Camera locked, her in mid-frame. Photoreal night rain.

### 08 · 金属作坊

She passes an open workshop: sparks from an angle grinder fly toward camera, a hammer strikes a steel plate twice. Camera slow lateral track left-to-right with her. Warm tungsten vs cold neon.

### 09 · 天桥

She climbs a rusted stair to an overpass. Below, a maglev or train rushes from right to left, Doppler-friendly. Camera low behind her, then tilts down to the train. One tilt.

### 10 · 停雨窗口（给 prompt 切换预留）

On the overpass she stops. Rain suddenly thins to drizzle, then almost stops. Neon still drips. Camera locked medium shot. Quiet city. Photoreal.（后续可由用户 prompt 加回雨声 / 远雷。）

### 11 · 暴雨回来

A wall of rain rolls down the street toward camera like a curtain. She starts running. Camera tracking backward in front of her, 28mm wide, rain hitting the lens. Photoreal.

### 12 · 巷战式追逐（无枪，可以是无人机）

A small search drone with a harsh white spotlight enters from the right alley, pans onto her. She ducks left through a doorway. Camera whip-ish pan right-to-left following the spotlight, then settle. Photoreal, no readable UI.

## Act 3 · 天台（3:00–4:15）· 镜头 13–17

### 13 · 上楼

Handheld-but-stable follow up a concrete stairwell, flickering tube lights, water dripping from pipes. Her boots hit metal steps. One continuous climb. Photoreal.

### 14 · 天台开门

Door slams open onto a rooftop helipad in the storm. Wind rips a loose tarp leftward. City of lights below. Camera push through the door with her. 24mm wide.

### 15 · 残骸

Wide: the capsule wreck sits on the next roof, still smoking, warning strobe blinking red. She walks toward it. Camera slow dolly with her. Rain, steam, red strobe as the only hard light.

### 16 · 接触事件

Close: her gloved hand grips a hot metal hatch, it groans, she pulls, a seal pops, white gas vents right. Camera locked ECU on hands then hatch. Photoreal mechanical VFX.

### 17 · 远处雷与飞船

Wide rooftop: lightning hits a tower on the LEFT of frame, a silent-looking shuttle on the RIGHT begins to spool engines, heat haze. She turns from left lightning to right ship. Camera slow pan left to right. Photoreal IMAX.

## Act 4 · 升空（4:15–5:00）· 镜头 18–20

### 18 · 引擎点火

Low angle under the shuttle: engine bells ignite, rain vaporizes into a white wall, loose gravel skitters toward camera. One locked wide. Photoreal, huge scale.

### 19 · 升空跟拍

Camera on the rooftop, tilt up as the shuttle lifts, city falling away, rain streaks, sonic-looking shock in puddles. She is a small figure in the lower third watching. Photoreal.

### 20 · 收束云层

The shuttle punches back through the same storm clouds from shot 01, becoming a bright point, then the clouds close. Slow crane up into clean moonlight above the storm. No text, no logo, photoreal IMAX end frame.
